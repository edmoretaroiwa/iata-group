Building leaders, one skill at a time

A youth development club for the skills schools don't always teach — public speaking, debate, music, preaching, and chess.

🎯 What is Alphas Club?

Alphas Club is a youth development initiative under IATA. It exists for one reason: to help young people develop the skills that make them stand out — in school, in their careers, and in life.

School teaches many important things. But there are some skills it doesn't always cover: how to speak confidently in front of an audience. How to defend an idea. How to play an instrument. How to lead. How to think strategically.

Alphas Club fills that gap — with five core areas of development.

⭐ What We Teach

Five skills. One club.

🎤

Public Speaking

Learn to speak with confidence — in front of any audience, anywhere.

💬

Debate

Sharpen your mind. Learn to argue, persuade, and think critically.

🎵

Music

Develop your musical talent — from singing to instruments.

📖

Preaching

Grow spiritually. Learn to share the Word with clarity and warmth.

♟️

Chess

Master strategy, patience, and the art of thinking ahead.

🌟

And More

The club grows with its members. New skills are always welcome.

❤️ Why It Matters

In Zimbabwe and across Africa, many young people have incredible potential — but not always the platform to develop it.

Alphas Club gives them that platform. It's a space where young people can:

  • Practise speaking without fear of judgment
  • Argue ideas respectfully and intelligently
  • Discover and nurture musical talent
  • Grow in faith and purpose
  • Learn strategy through the game of chess

These aren't just hobbies. They're the skills that build leaders, communicators, thinkers, and believers — the kind of people who change their communities.

⚙️ How It Works

👥

Weekly Meetups

Members gather regularly to practise, learn, and grow together in each of the five skill areas.

🏆

Events & Competitions

Members take part in debates, chess tournaments, speaking events, and music performances.

🎓

Mentorship

Senior members and coaches guide newer members — building confidence and skill at every step.

🌍

Community Impact

The club serves the community through outreach, performances, and service events.

🤝

Who Can Join?

Alphas Club is open to young people in Zimbabwe who want to grow — students, young professionals, anyone willing to learn and contribute.

Whether you're a seasoned debater or have never spoken in public before — you're welcome here.
