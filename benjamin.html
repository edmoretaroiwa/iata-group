🌱 Early Life

Benjamin Nyamukusa grew up in Chitungwiza — the same city as his future co-founder, Edmore Taroiwa.

They met at Seke 4 High School, where a friendship formed that would eventually become a partnership. Benjamin's calm character, steady support, and strong faith made him the perfect counterpart to Edmore's creative drive.

When Edmore first shared his vision of a free education platform for Africa, Benjamin didn't just listen — he believed in it. That belief is what made EduVille possible.

🤝 The Partnership

Two friends. One vision. One organization.

Every great project has a moment when it stops being one person's idea and becomes a team effort. For EduVille, that moment was when Benjamin told Edmore: "Let's build this together."

Benjamin brought something Edmore needed — a steady hand. Someone who could look at the big picture, ask hard questions, and keep the vision grounded. Together, they shaped the idea into something real:

  • A free education platform for African students
  • A youth club developing skills and character
  • An organization — IATA — built on faith

Partnership isn't just about sharing credit. It's about sharing the weight of building something from nothing. Benjamin carries that weight with grace, faith, and quiet strength.

✨ The Meaning of "Rsanta"

Benjamin is known to his friends and teammates as Rsanta — a name inspired by the word "saint", meaning someone set apart, holy, and devoted.

It fits him. In a world where young people often chase noise and attention, Benjamin carries himself with a quiet steadiness. He leads with faith. He keeps his word. He shows up when it matters.

"Be the kind of person others can build with."

The name Rsanta isn't a title he gave himself — it's a reflection of what his friends see in him.
