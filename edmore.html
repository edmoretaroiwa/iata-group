🌱 Early Life

Edmore Taroiwa grew up in Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe — a city filled with talent, ambition, and young people determined to build a better life.

He attended Seke 4 High School from Form 1, where he met his future co-founder Benjamin Nyamukusa. It was at Seke 4 that the friendship would grow into a partnership — and eventually an organization.

From an early age, Edmore showed two things: an obsession with creating, and a refusal to give up on hard problems.

🎵 Music Producer

Beats, creativity, and sound.

Edmore began producing music in Form 3 — using FL Studio Mobile, on nothing more than a phone.

While most people see a phone as a device for social media, Edmore saw a studio. He learned beats, rhythm, melody, and mixing — building his own sound from scratch, without expensive equipment or formal training.

That early experience taught him a lesson he carries into everything he builds today:

"You don't need much to create something great. You just need to start."

Music is not separate from his technology work — it's part of the same creative engine. It's where he learned to listen, to feel, and to iterate until something sounds right.

💻 The Developer Journey

In Form 5, Edmore asked a question that would change everything:

"What if there was a GitHub for education?"

That question became EduVille — a free education platform for African students.

He started building in Termux on Android. Then Firebase. Then GitHub Pages. Then Supabase. Every failure taught him something. Every bug made him better. Every line of code brought him closer to a working platform.

Today, EduVille is live at eduvillelearning.africa — serving students across Africa, completely free.
