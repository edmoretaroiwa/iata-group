📖 Our Story

IATA was born in Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe, in 2026 — from a friendship between two students at Seke 4 High School.

Edmore Taroiwa (known as TymerCRE) had spent years dreaming of a free education platform for African students. He built it — EduVille — with his friend Benjamin Nyamukusa (known as Rsanta). Together, they proved that even students with limited resources could build something the world could use.

But EduVille was not the destination — it was the beginning. The pair realized they needed a parent organization that could hold multiple projects and grow with them. That organization became IATA.

Tawananyasha Chipungumberi joined shortly after, expanding the team. Today, IATA is home to EduVille (education), Alphas Club (youth development), and future ventures yet to come.
