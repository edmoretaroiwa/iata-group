🌟 What is EduVille?

EduVille is a free education platform built for African students. It was created by Edmore Taroiwa (founder of IATA) to solve a problem he knew personally: many students in Zimbabwe and across Africa can't afford quality study materials.

EduVille puts lessons, videos, notes, past papers, and an AI tutor in one place — and gives it all away for free.

Originally built by Edmore alone, EduVille is now a flagship project under the IATA organization, alongside the Alphas Club.

📖 The EduVille Story

In Form 5, Edmore Taroiwa sat in his room at Chitungwiza and asked himself a question:

"What if there was a GitHub for education — a place where students could share notes, past papers, and tutorials freely?"

Then a second question:

"What if there was a TikTok for education — where teachers could stream tutorials and students could learn from short videos?"

Those two questions became EduVille.

With his best friend Benjamin Nyamukusa, Edmore started building. First on Termux on Android. Then on GitHub Pages. Then with Firebase. Then with Supabase. The platform grew — video lessons, study notes, past papers, an AI tutor, and progress tracking.

Today, EduVille lives at eduvillelearning.africa — and serves students across Zimbabwe, Botswana, and beyond.

🏫

Opening 1 March 2027

EduVille is not just an online platform. IATA is planning to open a physical EduVille school in Zimbabwe, bringing free or low-cost quality education to students who need it most.

Further details will be announced as we approach the opening date.

❤️ Why It Matters

In many parts of Africa, a student's success still depends on their family's income. Those who can afford tutors, textbooks, and private lessons have an advantage. Those who can't, fall behind — not because they lack talent, but because they lack access.

EduVille exists to change that.

No paywalls. No ads. No hidden costs. Just lessons, videos, notes, past papers, and an AI tutor — for everyone.
