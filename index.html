🚀 Our Projects

Two initiatives under the IATA umbrella.

🎓

EduVille

Live

A free education platform for African students studying ZIMSEC, Cambridge, and BEC. Video lessons, study notes, past papers, and an AI tutor called TymerCRE.

  • 📚 6 subjects — Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Studies
  • 🎬 Video lessons via YouTube
  • 🤖 TymerCRE — free AI study tutor, 24/7
  • 📊 Student progress tracking
  • 🌐 Live at eduvillelearning.africa
  • 🏫 Physical school opening 1 March 2027
Learn more →

Alphas Club

Active

A youth club for developing skills that schools don't always teach. Public speaking, debate, music, preaching, and chess.
